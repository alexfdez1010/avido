Ávido privacy policy

Updated 20 October 2026

Who is responsible

Ávido is a free Android reading app published by Alejandro Fernández Camello. It has no advertising, purchases, accounts or sign-in. Privacy and support contact: apps_alejandro@proton.me.

Your books and progress

Your optional profile name, preferences, goals, reading positions, history, statistics, XP and achievements are stored on your device. Ávido does not send them to the developer or operate a synchronization service. The app stores book metadata, extracted text and covers for display. Original books remain in your chosen folder. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

Google Play edition and APK catalogue

The Google Play edition has no Discover screen, online catalogue or book downloads, and has no internet permission. It reads only files you select through the system picker. The GitHub APK edition retains Discover. Only in that edition, the optional Discover screen connects directly over HTTPS to Project Gutenberg to browse its catalogue, search, display covers and download EPUBs. That service receives your IP address, search terms, selected language and book or cover requests, and may record those requests on its servers. Your profile, local reading history, statistics and personal book files are not transmitted. Project Gutenberg is an independent provider whose policy states that access logs, including IP addresses, are deleted within at most 60 days. Its practices and retention periods may change: consult its policy. We do not treat this processing as occurring only temporarily in memory. You can read local files without opening Discover.

Permissions and reminders

The system folder picker grants access to your chosen folder and supported subfolders. We do not request all-files access, contacts, camera, microphone, location or advertising identifiers. Optional reading reminders are scheduled on your device; we do not register a remote push token. Reminders may show a book title on your lock screen. Turn them off in Profile or Android settings.

Backups and security

There is no Ávido cloud. Android and your folder provider may back up or synchronize files depending on your settings. Ávido does not add its own storage encryption. Catalogue connections use HTTPS. Profile → Progress backup exports a JSON file containing positions, history, statistics, achievements, name and goals to a folder you choose. It excludes book contents, covers, paths and folder permissions. The backup is not encrypted; protect it and check whether your provider synchronizes it.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you replace it, clear app storage or uninstall, subject to Android backup and restore behavior. Deleting a book in the library also deletes its original file and cache; aggregate statistics and finished-book records may remain. Changing folders does not reset all data. Exported backups and books in shared folders may survive uninstalling and must be managed separately. There are no accounts to delete or remote reading histories held by the developer. Contact Project Gutenberg about catalogue access logs.

Support and your rights

If you email support, we receive your address and message through Proton Mail to respond. Do not attach books or progress backups unless needed and you choose to share them. We retain correspondence as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. Where applicable, request access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection or portability at apps_alejandro@proton.me. You may complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency. Handling support requests is based on responding to your request and our legitimate interest in providing support; legal obligations may require retaining certain data. External services may process data outside the European Economic Area under their own policies.

Age, links and changes

Ávido is intended for people aged 16 and over and is not designed for children under 16. It does not request ages or create children’s profiles. External links are governed by the destination’s policies; Ávido’s website is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose provider receives usual technical connection data. Books have their own rights: check availability in your country. We will update this page when data practices change and keep its revision date visible.
