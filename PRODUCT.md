# Ávido

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users

Readers who want to return to books instead of habitual scrolling. The launch website also addresses contributors evaluating an open source Android reader.

## Product Purpose

Make starting and resuming a book rewarding through optional reading goals, quests, XP, streaks and achievements. Success means supporting a reading habit chosen by the reader, not maximizing time in the app.

## Positioning

The owner wants to redirect the habit-forming techniques associated with attention-driven technology toward reading, with local storage and transparent source code. The approved launch voice is rebellious and provocative. Do not present addiction or habit effectiveness as a proven scientific outcome.

## Operating Context

An Android application reads a user-selected folder containing EPUB, PDF, TXT and Markdown files. Existing local books can be read offline. Discover accesses third-party services for public-domain book discovery and downloads. No iOS release is promised.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Spanish (Spain) and English app UI. Reader themes and typography, e-ink mode, saved reading position, reading statistics, quests and optional reminders. Reading progress is stored locally. No account, advertising or analytics integration is present in the audited application source. Local storage is not an encrypted vault; OS backups and third-party network requests require precise disclosure. The owner selected MIT for the source code.

## Brand Commitments

Ávido is the existing name. Preserve the app's recognizable warm paper, dark ink, green accents and expressive serif typography while giving the launch site a rebellious voice. The user authorized a bilingual GitHub Pages website, browser-language detection and publishing the completed work to main.

## Evidence on Hand

Application source under src/, existing design tokens in src/theme/, app icon under assets/images/, and Android verification documentation under docs/. There are no verified conversion, habit, customer or reading-effectiveness metrics to publish.

## Product Principles

- Put the reader's chosen books and reading ahead of engagement metrics.
- State privacy boundaries clearly and let the source be inspected.
- Use real capabilities and working release links as proof.
- Welcome contributors with reproducible development instructions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The app includes e-ink support and Atkinson Hyperlegible. The launch website must offer keyboard access, readable contrast, responsive layouts, reduced motion and an explicit Spanish/English switch.
