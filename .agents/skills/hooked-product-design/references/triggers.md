# Triggers: relevant cues at usable moments

Book basis: chapter 2 and loading the next trigger in chapter 5. Delivery controls are practical extensions.

## External trigger classes

| Type         | Typical role                               | Decision                                       |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Paid         | Purchased acquisition                      | Evaluate cost and useful activation            |
| Earned       | Press, discovery, featured placement       | Separate attention spikes from sustained value |
| Relationship | Recommendation, invitation, real reply     | Preserve sender intent and social trust        |
| Owned        | Icon, chosen subscription, permitted alert | Provide relevant repeat access and controls    |

Owned channels are not unlimited permission to interrupt. Historical examples sometimes infer permission from engagement; use actual channel permissions and user choices. Reading content is not consent to publicize browsing history.

## Trigger contract

Specify **need/event → eligibility and permission → usable time → channel → one clear action → destination → expiry/suppression → controls**. State available value before requesting attention. Distinguish user-scheduled intention from inferred predictions, and real replies from fabricated activity. Preserve promised destination through login when feasible.

## Transition to internal triggers

External cues can help users learn a reliable response to an existing need. Seek useful learned access, not escalating interruption. A visit without campaign attribution does not prove internal motivation: a calendar, shortcut, or untracked recommendation may be responsible.

For focused work, choose credible channels and timing. In workshops, explore unconventional cues while marking hardware, permissions, and feasibility assumptions. Creative exercises do not require adding sensors.

## Quality questions

Does the need exist now? Can the person act now? Is real value available? Is the next action clear? Has the task already been done? Is the channel welcome? Can the user pause/change cadence/stop? Is private context exposed?
