# Hooked Product Design

Turn a recurring user need into an easier, rewarding experience that improves with use. This skill contains original operational synthesis of _Hooked_ (2014), with practical extensions identified in the references. It supports diagnosis, design, writing, and implementation when requested.

## Work from the user's goal

1. Establish the target user, recurring situation, useful outcome, intended behavior, and natural cadence from available product documentation, flows, code, and evidence. Label assumptions; do useful work before asking for missing information.
2. Sketch one loop: **internal need → external cue, if needed → smallest action → meaningful reward → small investment → better next experience or next cue**. Investment may serve both purposes; do not invent one just to complete a diagram.
3. Locate the weakest supported transition. Improve ability before adding persuasion when motivation and a suitable cue already exist. Address value failure before increasing notifications or adding points.
4. Deliver concrete changes at the requested scope: screen/flow changes, exact proposed copy, prioritized work, or implementation. Connect each recommendation to evidence, its mechanism, a useful outcome metric, and a limit on undesirable effects.
5. Validate at the product's natural cadence. Activity counts alone do not establish a habit, user benefit, or causation.

Preserve the user's chosen product and scope. This skill alone does not authorize messaging users, changing live campaigns, collecting personal data, or publishing changes. Follow existing task authorization and repository instructions. In Ávido, read the app's instructions and product/design documents before implementation; this skill does not replace the UI system or the Impeccable design workflow.

## Essential limits

- Solve an existing need; do not manufacture anxiety, shame, scarcity, or dependency.
- Keep core outcomes reliable. Variability belongs in relevant discovery, social response, or skill challenge, not whether a promised service works.
- Ask for effort proportionate to earned value. Distinguish today's action from investment in tomorrow's experience.
- Make refusal, pause, recovery, and exit practical. A lost streak does not erase personal progress.
- Treat psychological mechanisms and numerical examples as hypotheses and historical illustrations, not guarantees or universal thresholds. Verify current empirical claims when material.
- Resources are in English; product copy follows its supported locale and requested voice. Examples are illustrative, not claims about existing functionality.
