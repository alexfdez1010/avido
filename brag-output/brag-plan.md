1. Product: an Android reader for local EPUB, PDF, TXT and Markdown books, with optional goals, streaks, quests and XP.
2. Strongest claim: Tu próximo vicio se lee / Your next obsession has pages.
3. Visual hook: huge Fraunces type, ink-black and paper fields, sharp mint underline.
4. Actual UI: source Android Today and reading screenshots, including the actual book and daily goal. The Today image uses demonstration data; its greeting is cropped out.
5. Shortest satisfying edit: 22 seconds.
6. Tone: polished, with a rebellious editorial opening about the business of scrolling.
7. Audio: restrained steady bed, three low-frequency soft hits and a discreet tap. No narration requested.
8. Share caption: Silicon Valley gana con tu scroll. Ávido pone las rachas, las misiones y las pequeñas victorias al servicio de tu próxima historia. Gratis, abierto y sin anuncios.
9. Flow: return to current local book → read at your own pace → see daily reading progress. Screens are genuine stills, with camera motion, not a recording of interaction.
