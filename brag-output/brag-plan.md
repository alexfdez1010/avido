# Ávido launch film

1. Product: an Android reader for local EPUB, PDF, TXT and Markdown books, with goals, streaks, quests, XP and achievements.
2. Strongest claim: Tu próximo vicio se lee / Your next obsession has pages.
3. Visual hook: huge Fraunces type and a direct opening about the profitable scroll economy.
4. Actual UI: five native screens per locale — library, statistics, trophies, home and reader. Spanish film uses Spanish app captures; English film uses English captures. Library and reader show genuine Gutenberg classics, including Don Quijote and Crime and Punishment / El crimen y el castigo.
5. Duration: 33 seconds. The user explicitly extended the original 22-second cut to give each screen room to read, overriding brag's default 15–25-second range.
6. Tone: polished editorial, provocative opening, natural product-led sequence.
7. Audio: existing steady instrumental bed and three soft low-frequency impacts. No narration.
8. Share caption: Silicon Valley gana con tu scroll. Ávido pone las rachas, las misiones y las pequeñas victorias al servicio de tu próxima historia. Gratis, abierto y sin anuncios.
9. Product flow: browse books → see reading history → celebrate progress → return to the current book → read.
