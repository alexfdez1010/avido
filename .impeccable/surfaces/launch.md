## Direction contract

THESIS: Reclaim the habits built by the attention economy and spend them on books. An editorial manifesto makes the idea immediate and the Android release actionable.
OWN-WORLD: Warm paper, near-black ink and sharp green; Fraunces display and Bricolage Grotesque interface from the app. Oversized literary typography and open editorial layouts, without decorative separator rules.
STORY: Recognize the scroll habit, discover the reading loop, inspect privacy boundaries, download or contribute.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact masthead above the dominant manifesto, Android download beneath it, and a tall localized native Today screen beside it. Keep the Spanish ending “vicio se lee” together. Signature interaction: a two-state comparison swaps the objective of the same habit loop, from attention extraction to reading progress; it respects reduced motion.
FORM: Candidate 6, the reader's marginal manifesto, seed 2994aa3d. The seven grounded forms were campaign poster, independent bookshop, paperback cover, library catalogue, reading notebook, marginal manifesto, and publishing colophon. The user-pinned rebellious voice and incumbent warm/green brand constrain the form. Challengers neon, glazier, Kraftwerk, Memphis, zine and oscilloscope lose product clarity; retain their discipline of continuous narrative, compositional clarity, purposeful state, expressive hierarchy, explanatory sequence and legible feedback respectively. Implement with code-native typography; no raster mockup is shipping proof.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Owner refinements

Marketing language takes priority over implementation details; technical limits
stay in linked documentation. The owner requested no full stops in headings,
no decorative separator rules, the real app icon, real app screenshots and an
accessible bilingual launch video. The approved current hero is “Tu próximo
vicio se lee” / “Your next obsession has pages”.

Seven native captures per locale use genuine Gutenberg classics and illustrative
reading history computed by the app. The six-image gallery includes detailed
statistics and unlocked trophies. The 33-second film uses a taller portrait
phone, with no subtitles, scene counters or secondary labels. Its simple native
player retains only the necessary music credit link beneath it.

The owner subsequently requested a carousel and closer video integration. The
film now leads the existing product showcase under “Cada página cuenta”, without
a separate duration heading. Six captures follow in one snapping horizontal
carousel with previous/next controls, native touch scrolling, keyboard navigation
and a screen-reader-only visible-range announcement. No automatic advancement.

On mobile, the owner requested exactly one capture at a time. Each slide fills
the track, with its image centered, no next-slide preview, and native horizontal
swiping that snaps to the adjacent capture. Desktop and tablet keep their layout.
